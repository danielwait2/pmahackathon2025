# GroupSync Demo Flow (Phase 6)

Use this exact flow in your presentation run-through.

1. Open landing page and highlight:
- Hero + survey pain-point stats.
- "Get Started Free" CTA.

2. Sign up with demo owner account and land on dashboard.

3. Create project using wizard:
- Step 1: Name, description, deadline.
- Step 2: Team expectations.
- Step 3: Copy invite link/code.

4. Join from second account:
- Open invite link in incognito.
- Sign up/login and join project.

5. Availability demo:
- Owner fills availability and saves.
- Member fills a different schedule and saves.
- Show team heatmap + meeting finder suggestions.

6. Tasks demo:
- Add one manual task.
- Run AI suggestions and add suggested tasks.
- Drag one task across board columns.
- Switch to list view on mobile breakpoint.

7. Team Agreement demo:
- Open Team tab and show agreement progress.
- Member clicks "I Agree".
- Owner edits agreement and confirm re-agree reset behavior.

8. Wrap-up:
- Show invite flow, mobile-ready task/availability UX, and agreement differentiator.
